Daiquiri

Shaken · Coupe Casa starter set
Made with a swap. Your aged rum stands in for white rum. A little richer, still lovely.

Ingredients

2 oz White rum Swap
1 oz Lime juice Have it
¾ oz Simple syrup Have it
1 Lime wheelGarnish, optional Missing

Method

  1. Add everything to a shaker with plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard until the tin is frosty, about 12 seconds.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe. Add the lime wheel if you have one.
